## Direction contract

THESIS: An instrument that certifies before it speaks. Ravora's page is a chronometer and its test certificate: a precision movement (dial, gears, jewel) shows Araiven working, and every claim arrives with its certificate (receipt, key check, rate table). Refuses the category pages: dark terminal with candles, centered gradient hero, three feature cards, stat row.

OWN-WORLD: Ground is Stone certificate paper (#eef1f5, rules at 14% ink) with large rounded Gunmetal "case" panels (radius 28 to 48px, inset from the viewport edge) holding the instruments, and exactly one full-bleed Amber panel for the risk guard. Amber is the jewel, the hand, the marker and the action colour; on paper, small Amber text uses Amber Deep #b45309. Type: Familjen Grotesk display (tight, 600 to 700), Albert Sans body, Martian Mono only for dial markings and tabular data. Components: dial with bezel ticks and a sweeping hand, meshing gears, certificate cards with perforated edges, hairline ledger rows, index lists. No glow, no gradient fills, no glass, no icon tiles, no kickers.

STORY: A visitor sees a precise instrument deciding a trade in real time (dial settles on 82, levels appear, reasoning types out), understands Ravora as a copilot that can be audited, watches a plain-English question become a scored plan, scrolls the movement that engages gate by gate and totals the receipt, sees their drawdown cap rule the amber panel, trusts the key-check certificate, then starts with paper money.
