# Ravora MVP V1

## Goal

Build a beautiful AI-powered wealth platform that demonstrates the core Ravora experience.

---

## Included Features

### User System

- Registration
- Login
- User Profile

---

### Dashboard

- Portfolio Overview
- AI Market Summary
- Opportunity Feed
- Goal Tracking

---

### Portfolio

- Holdings
- Allocation
- Performance Tracking

---

### AI Intelligence

- AI Market Summary
- AI Opportunity Discovery
- AI Explanations

---

### Risk Management

- Risk Profile
- Goal Setting
- Position Risk Information

---

### Paper Trading

- Virtual Balance
- Simulated Trades
- Performance Tracking

---

### Exchange Integration

Phase 1:
- Binance Connection

---

## NOT Included In V1

- Stocks
- Futures
- Options
- Forex
- Social Sentiment
- Mobile Apps
- Multi-Agent AI
- Autonomous Learning
- Full Auto Trading
- Institutional Features

---

## Success Criteria

A user can:

1. Sign up.
2. Connect Binance.
3. View portfolio.
4. Receive AI opportunities.
5. Understand AI reasoning.
6. Test ideas through paper trading.

---

## V1 Purpose

Validate demand.

Build trust.

Gather user feedback.

Prepare for V2 automation.
