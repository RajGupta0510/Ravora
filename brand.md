# Ravora

## Tagline

Your Wealth, Intelligently Managed.

Alternative:
AI Wealth Copilot for Modern Investors.

---

## Mission

Make intelligent wealth building accessible to everyone by combining artificial intelligence, market analysis, risk management, and automation into a simple and trustworthy experience.

---

## Vision

Create the world's most trusted AI-powered wealth platform where users can grow their money without needing to become professional traders.

---

## Target Audience

### Primary

- Beginners
- Busy professionals
- People with capital but limited trading knowledge
- Users who want opportunities without spending hours on charts

### Secondary

- Intermediate traders
- Investors looking for AI assistance
- Portfolio managers

## Design Direction

Theme:
Premium Financial Terminal & AI Wealth Copilot

Colors (Design System V2.0):
- **Gunmetal Night** (`#0b0e14`, `#10141d`, `#151a26`) - Deep canvas, primary surfaces, and focused dark atmosphere
- **Stone** (`#cbd5e1`, `#94a3b8`, `rgba(203, 213, 225, 0.12)`) - Neutral text, secondary surfaces, borders, and crisp contrast
- **Amber** (`#f59e0b`, `#fbbf24`, `#d97706`) - Signature accent for active intelligence, button CTAs, and key indicators

Style:
- Institutional Financial Terminal
- Impeccable Clean UI (No AI slop, no glow halos, no generic gradients)
- AI-Native Decision Architecture
- Professional Market Precision

Avoid:
- Crypto casino look
- Saturated purple/blue AI gradient slop
- Blurry radial glow blobs
- Unreadable low-contrast text
- Cyberpunk overload
